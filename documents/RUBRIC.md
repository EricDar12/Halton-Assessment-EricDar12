# Rubric

Scoring each category from **1 to 5**.

**Scale**
- **1** = Needs improvement  
- **3** = Meets expectations  
- **5** = Excellent  

---

## Scoring Table

| Category | 1 - Needs improvement | 3 - Meets expectations | 5 - Excellent |
|---|---|---|---|
| Communication & Clarity | Hard to follow; unclear structure | Clear enough; explains main points | Very clear; well-structured and concise |
| Problem-Solving Approach | Unstructured; limited iteration | Logical steps; explains choices | Methodical; evidence-driven; strong reasoning |
| Data Exploration & Understanding | Limited exploration; misses issues | Understands key fields; notes some issues | Strong understanding; validates assumptions; explains limitations |
| Technical Execution | Fragile or messy; hard to run | Works end-to-end; readable | Clean and reproducible; handles types/nulls well |
| Insight Quality | Trivial or weakly supported | Meaningful insights with support | Strong insights; well-supported; good judgment |
| Creativity & Curiosity | Minimal exploration | Explores a few angles | Explores multiple angles; creative but clear |
